# Brihat

Release files for Brihat. The app checks this page for new versions.

## Download

Open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and download the file for your device.

### Mac (Apple Silicon: M1 or later)

1. Download `Brihat-x.y.z.dmg` and open it.
2. Drag **Brihat** into **Applications**.
3. The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot check the app, because it is not yet signed with an Apple
   Developer certificate. Click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
   **Open Anyway** next to Brihat. Then open Brihat again. This is needed only once.

**Updates:** when a new version is out, Brihat shows **Update** on its home screen. Click it to download the new
installer, then drag Brihat into Applications again and choose **Replace**. Your data stays as it is.
